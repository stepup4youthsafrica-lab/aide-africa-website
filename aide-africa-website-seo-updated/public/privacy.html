Information we may collect

Depending on the services enabled, the website may process information you submit through enquiry forms or payment-provider checkout, such as your name, email address, donation amount and transaction reference.

How information may be used

Information should be used only for purposes explained to the individual, such as responding to enquiries, administering donations, issuing confirmations, meeting legal obligations and improving services.

Payments

Card and payment credentials should be entered only on the payment provider's hosted checkout. The website should not collect or store full card details. Stripe and PayPal will process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Retention and security

Set a documented retention schedule, limit access to authorised staff, use HTTPS, protect credentials and keep software updated. Keep only information that is necessary for the stated purpose or legally required.

Children's information and photography

Use appropriate safeguarding practices, parental/guardian consent where required, and informed consent for stories and images. Avoid publishing sensitive identifying information about children.

Insert the foundation's verified privacy contact, legal identity, applicable jurisdiction, complaint route and process for access or deletion requests before launch.
