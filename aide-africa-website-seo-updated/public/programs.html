What we do

Helping potential become progress.

Our work centres on education, community mobilisation and capability-building, social upliftment, and HIV/AIDS awareness and referral pathways. Youth-focused activities can complement these core areas where formally approved.

01 · Education & community learning

Promote education and community development learning, including household capacity-building and awareness activities tailored to local needs.

02 · Community mobilisation & capability-building

Support community dialogue structures, mobilise residents and develop community champions who can help strengthen local capacity.

03 · HIV/AIDS awareness & referral

Support education and awareness, and promote appropriate voluntary counselling and testing (VCT), PMTCT information and referral pathways for care and treatment.

04 · Youth talent and positive development

Where activities are approved and resourced, youth initiatives such as creative arts, innovation, sports and leadership can help young people discover and develop their strengths.

05 · Partnerships & community support

Work with schools, community groups, local leaders and responsible partners to strengthen delivery, referrals and community-led progress.
