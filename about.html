Who we are

We step. We teach. We empower.

AIDE Africa is the brand name of Step and Steppers Foundation Africa Co. Limited, a Uganda-registered company limited by guarantee working for social upliftment through education, community development and HIV/AIDS response.

Our organisation

Step and Steppers Foundation Africa Co. Limited was incorporated in Kampala, Uganda, on 4 April 2024. Registration number: 80034855614881. Founded by Sempija Michael and a team of committed subscribers, the organisation mobilises people and communities, builds their capabilities and works to uplift underserved and neglected communities.

AIDE Africa is the public-facing brand name. The registered entity remains Step and Steppers Foundation Africa Co. Limited.

Our mission

To mobilize people and communities, build their capabilities, and uplift the unprivileged and neglected through education, community development, capacity building, and the fight against HIV/AIDS.

Our vision

A resilient, educated, and healthy Africa where every community has the capability to overcome development challenges and no one is left behind.

What we do

  • Community mobilization and capability building: Support community dialogue structures and the development of trained community champions.
  • Social upliftment through education: Promote community development education and household capacity-building.
  • HIV/AIDS response: Support education, voluntary counselling and testing (VCT), prevention of mother-to-child transmission (PMTCT) awareness and referral pathways for care and treatment.

Our values in practice

  • Dignity: Respect the voice and rights of every person.
  • Integrity: Work responsibly and accountably.
  • Community: Mobilise people to shape local solutions.
  • Learning: Build capabilities through education and shared knowledge.
  • Partnership: Collaborate to address development challenges.

Our approach

Mobilise. Build capability. Uplift.

We aim to work alongside communities, schools, local leaders and partners to respond to needs, strengthen practical skills and connect people with relevant education and services.

  • Support community dialogue and mobilisation
  • Promote education and household capability-building

  • Work with partners and report progress responsibly
