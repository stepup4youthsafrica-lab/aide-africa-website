Who we are

Building pathways for young people to thrive.

A community-focused foundation working to connect young people with education, innovation, talent development and opportunity.

Our story

AIDE Africa / Step & Steppers Africa Foundation is presented here as a youth-focused nonprofit initiative with a commitment to helping young people recognise their strengths, develop practical skills and contribute positively to their communities.

This website is a launch-ready content framework. Before publication, the foundation should add its approved founding story, registration information, official leadership names and documented program history so supporters can verify the organisation and its work.

Our mission

To empower young people through education, innovation, mentorship and talent development, helping them build confidence, pursue opportunity and contribute to thriving communities.

Our vision

A future in which every young person has the support and opportunity to discover their potential and use it to make a positive difference.

Our values

  • Dignity: We respect the voice, potential and rights of every young person.
  • Integrity: We aim to act responsibly, transparently and accountably.
  • Inclusion: We work toward opportunities that welcome different backgrounds and abilities.
  • Innovation: We encourage curiosity, creativity and practical problem-solving.
  • Partnership: We believe lasting change grows through collaboration.
