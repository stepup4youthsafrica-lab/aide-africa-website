What we do

Helping potential become progress.

Our program framework brings together learning, creativity, practical skills, mentorship and positive youth development.

01 · Education & school engagement

Support learning opportunities, school-based engagement and activities that encourage young people to keep learning. Program activities and eligibility should be confirmed locally before registration opens.

02 · Innovation & technology

Encourage digital confidence, design thinking, creativity and practical problem-solving through workshops and collaborative challenges.

03 · Creative arts & talent development

Provide pathways for young people to discover, practise and showcase talents in areas such as music, performance, visual arts and other creative disciplines.

04 · Sports, wellbeing & teamwork

Use inclusive activities to encourage teamwork, discipline, healthy participation and a sense of belonging.

05 · Mentorship & leadership

Connect young people with positive role models, guidance and opportunities to practise communication, decision-making and leadership.
