Our partnership principles

Clear goals. Shared responsibility. Honest reporting.

  • Agree on objectives and the intended beneficiaries
  • Set realistic deliverables, budgets and timelines
  • Protect children, participants and personal information
  • Measure progress and communicate challenges
  • Recognise contributions accurately and transparently

We will discuss each opportunity and confirm capacity, governance requirements and appropriate agreements before committing to delivery.
